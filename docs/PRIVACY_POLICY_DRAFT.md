## 2. What we collect
**When you register (you provide):** full name; mobile number; a password (stored only as a one-way hash); a driver selfie
taken with your phone's camera (required); vehicle type and vehicle registration number (not required for cycles);
optionally email, date of birth, current/previous company, role and experience, working city and area, UPI ID and
Google Pay / PhonePe number, and a referral code.

**When you use campaigns:** campaign join requests, the version and time of the campaign terms you accept, T-shirt size and
pickup choices, and the Morning / Evening / Night campaign photos you take with the camera (these may show your vehicle and
its number plate).

**Location (only if you choose to record a route):** precise GPS location, from the moment you tap *Start Route* on a live
campaign day until you tap *End Route* or the campaign day ends. If you allow it, this continues while the app is closed or
the screen is off; a notification is shown the whole time. We do not collect location at any other time.

**Payments:** records of payouts we make to you (amount, date, UPI reference).

We do not collect your contacts, messages, call logs, files, microphone audio, advertising ID or browsing data, and the app
contains no advertising or analytics tools.

## 3. Why we use it
To create and manage your account; to verify riders (including by looking at your selfie); to decide which campaigns you can
join; to check campaign delivery (photos and, if recorded, routes); to calculate and pay your earnings and referral rewards;
to send you app notifications about your account and campaigns; to prevent fraud; and to meet legal and accounting duties.

## 5. How long we keep it
While your account is active. When you delete your account (see below), we delete it, except records we need for payouts,
accounting, tax and dispute handling: your name, Rider ID, mobile number, vehicle type and number, the campaign photos you
submitted, payment/payout records and the campaign terms you accepted. [STATE HOW LONG THESE ARE KEPT, e.g. 8 years for
accounting records.]

## 6. Deleting your account
In the app: *Profile → Delete Account*. Without the app: https://flexriders.in/delete-account. If you never took part in a
campaign or received a payment, everything is deleted. Otherwise your login is disabled and your selfie, email, date of
birth, work details, area, payment details (UPI / Google Pay / bank), route (GPS) data, documents and notifications are
erased; the records in section 5 are kept.

## 7. Security
All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Files are kept in private storage that is not publicly
accessible; the admin dashboard requires staff login. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] if you
notice a problem.
